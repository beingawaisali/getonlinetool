When to Use Each Case

Sentence case is the standard for body text, emails, and most writing. It capitalizes only the first letter of each sentence.

Title Case is used for headings, article titles, book titles, and headlines. Every major word starts with a capital letter.

UPPER CASE is used for emphasis, acronyms, warning labels, or shouting on the internet.

lower case is often used in informal writing, poetry, brand names (like "adidas"), and social media.

Alternating case and Inverse case are used for humour, memes, or stylistic creative writing.
