How to Use the Upper to Lower Case Converter

Converting uppercase text to lowercase takes just a few seconds with this tool.

  1. Paste your text into the Input Text box. It can be fully uppercase, mixed case, or anything in between.
  2. Click "Convert to Lowercase" — the result appears instantly in the Output box.
  3. Copy the result using the Copy button and paste it wherever you need it.

What Gets Converted?

Every uppercase letter (A–Z) in your text is converted to its lowercase equivalent (a–z). Everything else — numbers, punctuation, spaces, symbols, and line breaks — is left completely unchanged. So a text like "HELLO, World! 123" becomes "hello, world! 123".

When Would You Need This?

There are many situations where converting uppercase text to lowercase is useful. You may have received text typed entirely in capitals and need to reformat it. You may be writing code where variable names must be lowercase. You may be cleaning up data exported from a database where fields were stored in uppercase. Or you may have accidentally left Caps Lock on while typing and want a quick fix without retyping everything.
