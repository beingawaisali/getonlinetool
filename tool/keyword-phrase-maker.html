How to Use the Keyword Phrase Maker

This keyword phrase tool takes words from up to three separate lists and combines them into every possible phrase combination. If you run Google Ads campaigns, you'll know exactly why that matters - covering every combination of services, locations, and descriptors by hand is the kind of work that eats an entire afternoon.

  1. Enter your word lists - type or paste one word or phrase per line into each of the three input boxes. List 3 is optional; leave it empty and only Lists 1 and 2 get combined.
  2. Choose a separator - pick how the words should be joined: Space (default), Nothing (words merged together), or Dash (-).
  3. Select match types - tick one or more Google Ads keyword match type formats, or grab them all at once with the "Select All" checkbox.
  4. Click "Make Keyword List" - every possible combination across your lists gets generated instantly in the formats you picked.
  5. Copy your results - hit "Copy Results" and paste straight into Google Ads or your keyword planner.

What Are Google Ads Keyword Match Types?

A keyword match type controls which searches are allowed to trigger your ads. Get this wrong and you either waste budget on irrelevant clicks or miss searches you wanted. Here's a quick breakdown of the four formats this tool supports:

Match TypeFormatWhat it does
Broad Match keyword Ads can show on searches related to your keyword, including synonyms and variations.
Modified Broad +keyword Ads show when searches include the exact terms marked with +, in any order.
Phrase Match "keyword" Ads show on searches that include your phrase in the same order, with possible words before or after.
Exact Match [keyword] Ads show only on searches that match your exact keyword or very close variations.

Do the maths on a typical campaign and the problem becomes obvious. Say you're advertising a business with 5 services across 4 locations, each with 3 descriptors - that's 60 unique keyword phrases. This keyword phrase generator does the whole thing in seconds. Paste your lists, pick your formats, click once.
