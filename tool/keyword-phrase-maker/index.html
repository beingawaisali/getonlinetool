How to Use the Keyword Phrase Maker

The Keyword Phrase Maker combines words from up to three separate lists into every possible phrase combination. This is especially useful for Google Ads campaigns where you need to cover all combinations of services, locations, and descriptors.

  1. Enter your word lists — Type or paste one word or phrase per line into each of the three input boxes. List 3 is optional; if left empty, only Lists 1 and 2 will be combined.
  2. Choose a separator — Select how you want words from each list to be joined: Space (default), Nothing (words merged together), or Dash (-).
  3. Select match types — Choose one or more Google Ads keyword match types. You can select all at once using the "Select All" checkbox.
  4. Click "Make Keyword List" — The tool will instantly generate every possible combination across your lists in the selected match type formats.
  5. Copy your results — Click "Copy Results" and paste directly into Google Ads or your keyword planner.

What Are Google Ads Keyword Match Types?

Google Ads uses match types to control which searches trigger your ads. Here is a quick breakdown of the four match types this tool supports:

Match TypeFormatWhat it does
Broad Match keyword Ads can show on searches related to your keyword, including synonyms and variations.
Modified Broad +keyword Ads show when searches include the exact terms marked with +, in any order.
Phrase Match "keyword" Ads show on searches that include your phrase in the same order, with possible words before or after.
Exact Match [keyword] Ads show only on searches that match your exact keyword or very close variations.

Why Use a Keyword Combination Tool?

If you are running Google Ads for a business with multiple services, locations, and descriptors, manually combining all variations is extremely time-consuming. For example, combining just 5 services × 4 locations × 3 descriptors gives you 60 unique keyword phrases. Multiply that by four match types and you have 240 keywords to write by hand.

The Keyword Phrase Maker handles this automatically in seconds, saving hours of tedious copy-pasting work. It is completely free, works in your browser, and requires no account or installation.
