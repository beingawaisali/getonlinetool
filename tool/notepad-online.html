About This Online Notepad

This is a simple, fast, and distraction-free online text editor. Everything you type is saved automatically to your browser's Local Storage — so even if you accidentally close the tab or refresh the page, your notes will still be there when you come back.

There is no account to create, no files to manage, and no cloud service involved. Your notes stay entirely on your own device.

Features

  1. Auto-save — your text is saved instantly as you type, with no manual save button needed.
  2. Font size control — adjust the text size from 14px to 24px for comfortable reading or writing.
  3. Copy All — copy your entire notepad content to the clipboard in one click.
  4. Download as .txt — save your notes as a plain text file to your device.
  5. Live stats — word count, character count, lines, and reading time update as you write.
