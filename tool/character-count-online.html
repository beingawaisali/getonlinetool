How to Use the Character Count Tool

This character checker doesn't need any setup. Type or paste your text into the box and every count updates live, no button press needed. Since it doubles as a word counter tool, you get characters, words, sentences, paragraphs, lines, and reading time all in one place.

Paste your content, glance at the numbers, and adjust as you go. Whether you're trimming a headline down to size or checking a caption before you post it, the count is right there in front of you the whole time you're typing.

Characters With Spaces vs Without Spaces

Characters with spaces includes every character - letters, numbers, punctuation, and spaces. This is what most platforms like Twitter or Google use for their character limits. Characters without spaces excludes all spaces, showing only the raw content length. Knowing the difference matters more than it seems - a caption that looks fine with spaces counted might read completely differently once you strip them out, especially for platforms that measure things strictly.
