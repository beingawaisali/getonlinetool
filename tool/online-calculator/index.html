How to Use the Online Calculator

This calculator works exactly like a standard handheld calculator — click buttons or use your keyboard to enter numbers and operators.

  1. Click number buttons or press keys on your keyboard to enter numbers.
  2. Select an operator — addition (+), subtraction (−), multiplication (×), or division (÷).
  3. Press = or Enter to see the result. The calculation is saved in the History panel on the right.
  4. Use AC to clear everything, +/− to toggle the sign, % to convert to a percentage, and the backspace button to delete the last digit.
