How to Use the Online Calculator

Works like any calculator you've used before. Click the buttons or just type on your keyboard, whichever's easier.

  1. Enter numbers by clicking or typing.
  2. Pick an operator, plus, minus, times, or divide.
  3. Hit = or Enter for the result, and it lands in the History panel on the right automatically.
  4. AC clears everything, +/- flips the sign, % converts to a percentage, and backspace deletes the last digit if you make a typo.
