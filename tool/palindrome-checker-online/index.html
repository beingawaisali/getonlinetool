What Is a Palindrome?

A palindrome is a word, phrase, number, or sequence of characters that reads the same forward and backward. The word "racecar" is a classic example — spell it forwards or backwards and it is identical. Famous palindrome phrases include "A man, a plan, a canal: Panama" and "Was it a car or a cat I saw?" — both read the same in either direction when spaces and punctuation are ignored.

How to Use the Palindrome Checker

  1. Type or paste your text into the input box. The check runs live as you type.
  2. Adjust check options in the sidebar — you can choose whether to ignore case, spaces, and punctuation.
  3. Read the result — the tool will tell you clearly whether your text is a palindrome or not, and show you the reversed version for comparison.
  4. Try the examples in the sidebar to see how famous palindromes look.
