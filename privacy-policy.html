Privacy Policy

Straightforward answers to the question: What happens to your data when you use GetOnlineTool? The short version is, very little. Here is the long version.

Last updated: January 2026

Our Approach to Privacy

GetOnlineTool was built on a simple belief: a free online tool should not cost you your privacy. The majority of the tools on this website run completely in your web browser. This means that when you paste text into a word counter, a case converter or a text reverser the processing is done on your own device and the text is not uploaded to us or anyone else. This policy explains what that looks like in practice, along with the limited data that any website inevitably touches.

Information You Enter Into Our Tools

Any text, numbers, or content you type or paste into any tool on GetOnlineTool is processed locally in your browser. We don’t get it, we don’t store it on a server, and we don’t share it.” Tools like the online notepad, may store your content in your browser’s local storage so it is there when you come back. That storage is on your own device and is not sent to us. You may delete it at any time using the clear option of the tool itself or your browser settings.

Information We May Automatically Collect

Like almost every website, our hosting and analytics systems may record a small amount of standard technical information when you visit. This can include:

  • The site uses basic log data (your browser type, device type and the pages you view) to keep the site running and understand what tools are useful to people.
  • Approximate location at the country or city level, derived from your IP address, never a precise address.
  • Referral information showing whether you arrived from a search engine, a link, or directly.

This information is aggregated and used to improve the site. It is not used to personally identify you, and we do not sell it.

Cookies and Analytics

We may use privacy-conscious analytics to see how the site is used in general terms, such as which tools are popular and where visitors run into trouble. If cookies are used, they are limited to this purpose. You are always free to block or delete cookies through your browser settings without losing access to any tool on the site, since the tools themselves do not depend on them.

Third-Party Services

Some parts of the site rely on trusted third parties, for example web fonts loaded from a font provider or analytics from an analytics provider. These services may process limited technical data as described in their own privacy policies. We keep third-party integrations to a minimum and choose them with privacy in mind.

Advertising

If we display advertising in the future, ad partners may use cookies or similar technologies to show relevant ads. Where this happens, we will update this policy and, where required, provide the appropriate controls and disclosures. As of the date above, our focus remains on keeping tools clean and useful.

Data Security

Because most of your activity never leaves your browser, there is very little sensitive data for anyone to intercept in the first place. For the standard technical data described above, we rely on secure, encrypted connections (HTTPS) and reputable hosting to keep information protected in transit and at rest.

Children's Privacy

GetOnlineTool is a general-audience website and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us and we will address it promptly.

Your Choices and Rights

You are in control. You can clear any locally saved content through the relevant tool or your browser. You can block cookies. And you can contact us to ask questions about how your data is handled. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA, including the right to access or request deletion of personal data we may hold. We honour valid requests in line with applicable law.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time as the site grows or as regulations change. When we do, we will revise the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be made clear so you are never caught off guard.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or how your data is handled, we would be glad to help. Reach out through our Contact Us page and a real person will get back to you.
