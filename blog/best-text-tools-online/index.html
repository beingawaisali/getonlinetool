5 Best Text Tools Online That Save You Time

8 November 2026 · 3 min read

Nobody gets into writing because they love counting words. But the real time sink is everything wrapped around the writing, the fiddly bits that quietly swallow an afternoon. Client wants 800 words, you've got 950, and it's due at five. A paste from a PDF comes out riddled with gaps. A headline looks off and you can't say why. Free text tools fix all of that in seconds. These are the five I'd bookmark first.

1. Word Count

Open this one first. Honestly, it'll probably be the tab that never closes. Paste a draft into the Word Count tool and watch words, characters and sentences tick up while you type. Student with a page limit hanging over you? The Essay Word Counter even guesses your page count. Over the limit? Reduce Word Count lets you set a target and points at the fluff you can lose. You'll be surprised how many "really" and "very" are hiding in there.

2. Case Maker

Headline capitals are weirdly tricky. Does "to" get a capital? Depends who you ask. Argue about it all you like, or let the Case Maker flip your text to title case, sentence case and a few others with one click. Just want a plain flip? Upper to Lower Case and Lower to Upper Case are blunt instruments, and sometimes that's all you need. For headlines, though, go with the Title Case Maker. It knows "of" and "the" shouldn't shout.

3. Space Remover

You've copied a paragraph out of a PDF and it's full of double spaces. Or there's a sneaky space at the end of a spreadsheet cell and your formula refuses to work. Annoying, right? The Space Remover can wipe every space, squash the doubles, or trim just the ends. It's tiny, but on a long list it saves you a ridiculous amount of clicking.

4. Remove Empty Lines

Emails and web pages are full of blank lines nobody asked for. Copy three paragraphs and you get a canyon between each one. Remove Empty Lines clears the lot, or leaves a single gap between paragraphs so it still reads like writing. Use it straight after the Space Remover and a messy paste is tidy in about a minute.

5. Character Replacer

Picture find and replace after a few weeks at the gym. Line up a few rules in the Character Replacer (change one word, swap a dash for a comma, bin a symbol) and it works through them in one go. Great for cleaning exported data. Also great for the day you notice you've spelled a brand name wrong forty times. We've all been there.

A few more worth keeping close

Some tools miss the top five but still deserve a spot. Character Count is perfect for tweets and meta tags with strict limits. Notepad Online is a plain scratchpad that saves in your browser. And the Comma Inserter turns a column of items into one comma separated line, or adds thousand separators to big numbers.

Why bother with browser tools?

For starters, there's nothing to install. It all happens in the browser tab, which also means your draft never leaves your device. Handy if it isn't public yet. And yes, everything works fine on a phone, so you can fix a headline from the sofa.

Start with the chore that annoys you most, and add the others whenever you like. Everything is free at GetOnlineTool.
