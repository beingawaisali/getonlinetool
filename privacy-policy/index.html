Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026.

This is a simple site without logins, accounts, or payment forms, so there isn't much personal data for us to collect in the first place. Here's what actually happens when you use GetOnlineTool.

How the tools handle your data

All the tools on this site (word counter, case converter, calculator, and the rest) run entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Whatever text or numbers you type into a tool stay on your device. None of it is sent to our servers, stored in a database, or shared with anyone. Close the tab or refresh the page, and that data is simply gone.

One exception is the Notepad tool, which saves your text in your browser's local storage so it's still there the next time you open the page on the same device and browser. That data never leaves your device either, and you can clear it any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Server logs

Like most websites, our server logs basic technical information when you visit: things like your browser type, an approximate location based on IP address, and which pages were requested. This is standard web hosting information used only to keep the site running and catch technical problems. It isn't linked to your identity.

Fonts

We use Google Fonts to display the text on this site, which means your browser makes a request to Google's servers to load them. Google may log that request as part of its own service. We don't control what Google does with that data; its own privacy policy covers it.

What we don't do

We don't use tracking cookies, we don't sell data (there isn't any to sell), and we don't run third-party advertising scripts that build a profile of you.

Changes to this policy

If we ever add anything that changes this, like accounts, ads, or analytics tools, this page will be updated to reflect it, and the date at the top will change.

Questions

If you have questions about privacy or want to know more about how a specific tool works, email support@getonlinetool.com and we'll answer honestly.
