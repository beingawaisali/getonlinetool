Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026.

Quick version: there's no login here, no account, no payment form, so there's genuinely not much of your data floating around to worry about. But here's the actual breakdown anyway, since "trust us" isn't really good enough on its own.

How the tools handle your data

Every tool on this site runs in your browser. The word counter, case converter, calculator, all of it. Whatever you type stays right there on your device. It doesn't get sent to a server, doesn't get saved in a database somewhere, and doesn't get shared with anyone. Refresh the page and it's gone, simple as that.

Notepad's a bit different since it saves your text in your browser's local storage, so it's still there next time you open the page on the same device. Still never leaves your device though. Clear your browser data whenever and it's wiped.

Server logs

Like basically every website out there, the server sees some basic stuff when you visit. Browser type, a rough location from your IP, which page you asked for. Standard hosting stuff, nothing tied to who you actually are, and nobody's sitting there reading it line by line either.

Fonts

Fonts come from Google Fonts, which means your browser pings their servers to load them. That's on Google's end, not ours, so their own privacy policy covers whatever they do with that request.

What we don't do

No tracking cookies. No selling data, there isn't any worth selling anyway. No ad scripts building a profile on you in the background. None of that stuff is running here, and there's no plan to add it either.

Changes to this policy

If that ever changes, if accounts or ads or analytics get added down the line, this page gets updated and the date at the top moves too.

Questions
